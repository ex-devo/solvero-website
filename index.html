About Solvero

Solvero is a business and technology consultancy based in Johannesburg. We work remotely with businesses internationally, helping them make sense of complex problems and find a simpler way forward.

Our mission

To make complex business problems easier to understand and turn that understanding into practical improvements.

Our ethos

We ask why. What is a process meant to achieve? Does the reason behind it still apply? What could work better?

We believe simplicity is the ultimate sophistication. Finding it takes a clear understanding of the business and the people doing the work. We use plain language and visuals to make complexity understandable, so people can question assumptions and make decisions together.

We explain our recommendations and take responsibility for carrying the agreed work through to delivery.

Our background

Solvero brings almost two decades of technology and enterprise experience.

Solvero grew out of seeing how established processes can persist long after the constraints that shaped them have changed. People inherit ways of working, while daily demands and urgent problems leave little time to reconsider them. Solvero exists to give that work dedicated attention and carry useful improvements into practice.

How we work

Explore your challenges and objectives

We start with a conversation about your business, the challenges you face and what you’d like to achieve. We help you make sense of the options and recommend a practical way to resolve those challenges and achieve your objectives. Together, we agree the work, responsibilities and how we’ll check the result.

Deliver, test and hand over

We deliver the agreed process or system changes and test them against the requirements, including exceptions and failure cases. We review the result with you before handover, with documentation and clear ownership for using it.
