Solution Design & Delivery

Get a working solution to a defined business problem.

Our Solution Design & Delivery service connects business needs with implementation. We define what the solution must do, build the agreed tool or system connection and test it with the people who will use it.

Discuss the solution you need

Carry the requirement through to something people can use.

You know what the business needs to achieve, but the route into delivery is unclear. Requirements leave room for different interpretations, system dependencies surface late or a finished feature still leaves people copying information by hand. The missing connection is often between the business process and the implementation decisions.

We work through the task, inputs and intended result with your team. We examine existing systems, access constraints and exceptional cases before agreeing the design. Requirements and acceptance criteria describe the business behaviour the solution must support, including what happens when an update fails or information is incomplete.

A solution defined, built and checked for use.

A clear design and delivery scope

Agreed requirements, system responsibilities and acceptance criteria. The design identifies dependencies, operating boundaries and the decisions your team needs to make before delivery.

The agreed working solution

A business tool, integration, reporting workflow or automation built around the chosen scope. We assess the existing systems and select a practical delivery approach for the task.

Test evidence and a usable handover

Checks covering everyday work and important failure cases, followed by acceptance with your team. Documentation explains the solution, operating responsibilities and the support arrangements you agree.

How we work with your team.

  1. 01

    Define the behaviour

    Use real examples to agree inputs, outcomes and acceptance criteria. Check system access, dependencies and the decisions needed before building.

  2. 02

    Build and check the solution

    Implement the agreed design and test normal, missing-information and failure cases. Review the result with the people doing the work and address gaps against the scope.

  3. 03

    Introduce and hand over

    Agree how the solution will enter use, who owns it and how issues are handled. Hand over the operating documentation and check the business outcome against the agreed measures.

Connect the business task to the right implementation.

The solution might connect customer records between applications, prepare a recurring report, collect information through a purpose-built form or automate a defined operational task. AI can support suitable interpretation tasks when the inputs, checking effort and decision boundaries justify it. The delivery approach follows the problem.

We can own an agreed delivery scope or work alongside your existing development team. When working with a team, we clarify requirements, resolve business questions and check acceptance together. We define responsibility for environments, deployment, third-party services and ongoing support before committing to delivery.

For suitable technical changes, explore our business process automation and integration service.

Plan the work with confidence.

Can you work with our existing development team?

Yes. We can help define behaviour and acceptance criteria, resolve difficult handoffs or deliver an agreed component alongside your team. We establish responsibilities and access at the start.

Will the solution use AI?

We assess the task and compare suitable methods. Explicit rules, an existing application feature, a system integration or AI may each fit different parts of the work. The choice follows the required result and operating constraints.

Can you work with our existing systems?

We first check the relevant interfaces, permissions and system responsibilities. The agreed design reflects what those systems can reliably support and identifies any access or vendor dependencies.

How do you agree the cost and timeline?

We assess the required behaviour, integrations, exception cases and delivery responsibilities. We then propose the scope and commercial terms, making dependencies and any further investigation explicit.

Can you work with our team internationally?

Yes. We are based in Johannesburg and work remotely with English-speaking businesses internationally. We agree meeting times, a shared place for working documents and the people who can make decisions. Discovery uses recent examples and walkthroughs with your team. Access, testing, acceptance and handover are agreed for the scope.
