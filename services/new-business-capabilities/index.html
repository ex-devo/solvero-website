Turn a business goal into a capability people can use.

These examples describe possible engagements. We define the actual scope and implementation around your business goal.

Customer self-service

Give customers a new way to submit information, make a request or follow its progress. We define the journey and deliver the agreed forms, rules and system connections.

Support for a new service or product

Put the operating process and system behaviour in place for a new offering. We clarify the rules, information needs and handoffs before implementing the agreed changes.

New partner or application connections

A new business application

A capability defined, delivered and ready for its agreed use.

Requirements and a solution design

The business rules, system behaviour, data and interfaces the solution needs to support. We document the architecture decisions and acceptance criteria so business and technical teams can work from the same design.

A working solution

The agreed process changes, configured software, custom application or integration, ready for the intended users. We build around the requirements and the systems the solution needs to work with.

Testing and handover

Checks covering expected behaviour, missing information and important failure cases. Your team reviews the result against the acceptance criteria, with documentation and clear ownership for using it.

How we work with your team.

  2. 02

Build around the business requirement.

We compare existing software services (SaaS) with custom development, looking at fit, integration, cost and the ability to adapt as needs change. The solution may combine both.

You can engage us for business or systems analysis, solution architecture, implementation, or a combination. We can deliver a defined scope or work alongside your team. We agree who owns the decisions, development, testing and acceptance before work begins.

Business analysis, systems analysis and solution architecture.

We use systems analysis techniques such as use case modelling, data modelling and interface analysis to define how the solution should behave. Our solution architecture work sets out how applications, APIs and data will fit together. The resulting requirements, data mappings and interface specifications give business and technical teams a shared design to review.

Plan the work with confidence.

Can we start with a business goal rather than a specification?

Yes. We help define the required behaviour, assess options and agree a useful delivery scope. You do not need to arrive with a chosen platform or a finished set of requirements.

Can you work with our existing systems and development team?

Yes. We check the existing interfaces, permissions and system responsibilities, then agree how the solution will fit. We can own a defined part of the analysis, design or implementation and work through business decisions and acceptance with your team.

Will we need new software, automation or AI?

We assess the requirement before recommending an approach. The solution might use existing software, a custom application, an integration or automation. Where AI is useful, we define the task and the checks its output needs.

How do you agree cost and timing?

We assess the required behaviour, dependencies, exceptional cases and delivery responsibilities. We then propose the scope and commercial terms, making any further investigation explicit.

Can you work with our team internationally?

Yes. We are based in Johannesburg and work remotely with English-speaking businesses internationally. We agree working times, decision-makers and a shared place for documents, then plan access, testing and handover around the engagement.
