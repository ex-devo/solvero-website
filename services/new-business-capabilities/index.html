New Business Capabilities

Make something new possible for your business.

Introduce a service or way of working that your current processes and systems cannot support. We work with your team to define what is needed, design the solution and deliver the agreed capability through testing and handover.

Discuss the capability you need

Give your next business goal a practical route into use.

You may want customers to submit requests online, connect a new partner or support a new product. The goal is clear, but the required processes, information and technology still need to be worked out. We connect that ambition to a defined delivery scope.

We establish who needs the capability, what they need to do and how success will be checked. We assess existing systems, business rules and dependencies, compare suitable options and define requirements and acceptance criteria with your team.

Turn a business goal into a capability people can use.

These examples describe possible engagements. We define the actual scope and implementation around your business goal.

Customer self-service

Give customers a new way to submit information, make a request or follow its progress. We define the journey and deliver the agreed forms, rules and system connections.

Support for a new service or product

Put the operating process and system behaviour in place for a new offering. We clarify the rules, information needs and handoffs before implementing the agreed changes.

New partner or application connections

Enable a new exchange of information between your business and another system or partner. We agree record ownership, interfaces, permissions and the behaviour when an update fails.

A new business tool

Give your team a purpose-built way to capture information, prepare a report or complete a task it cannot currently support. We define and test the useful behaviour with its users.

A capability defined, delivered and ready for its agreed use.

A clear design and delivery plan

Agreed requirements, process and system responsibilities, dependencies and acceptance criteria. We identify the decisions and access needed before building.

The working capability

The agreed process, tool, integration or software change that lets the business do something new. We select a practical approach around the requirement and the systems it needs to work with.

Testing and handover

Checks covering expected behaviour, missing information and important failure cases. We work through acceptance with your team and document how the capability is operated and supported.

How we work with your team.

  1. 01

    Define what needs to become possible

    Work through the business goal, intended users and real scenarios. Agree the required behaviour, priorities and delivery boundaries.

  2. 02

    Design and build the capability

    Compare implementation options, establish the process and system design, and build the agreed solution. Resolve requirements and dependencies with your team as the work progresses.

  3. 03

    Test and introduce it

    Check the capability against the agreed scenarios, prepare the handover and work with the people who will use and own it. Define any further support in the scope.

Build around the business requirement.

The capability may use an existing application feature, an integration, a purpose-built tool or a combination of process and software changes. AI can support suitable tasks when the inputs, checking effort and decision boundaries justify it. The required result guides the choice.

We can deliver an agreed scope or work alongside your existing delivery team. Responsibilities for environments, deployment, third-party services, acceptance and ongoing support are defined before implementation. The engagement can include analysis and planning where the goal is clear but the requirements still need to be established.

Work out what the business needs to be able to do.

We assess the gap between the current operation and the capability you want to introduce. That includes the people, processes, information and systems involved, with dependencies and practical options made explicit. The resulting plan sets out how the agreed capability will be delivered.

Plan the work with confidence.

Can we start with a business goal rather than a specification?

Yes. We help define the required behaviour, assess options and agree a useful delivery scope. You do not need to arrive with a chosen platform or a finished set of requirements.

Can you work with our existing systems and development team?

We check the relevant interfaces, permissions and ownership before agreeing the design. Where your team is involved, we define responsibilities, resolve business questions and check acceptance together.

Will the solution use AI or automation?

We compare methods against the task. Existing application features, explicit rules, an integration, automation or AI may fit different parts of the capability. The choice follows the required result and operating constraints.

How do you agree cost and timing?

We assess the required behaviour, dependencies, exceptional cases and delivery responsibilities. We then propose the scope and commercial terms, making any further investigation explicit.

Can you work with our team internationally?

Yes. We are based in Johannesburg and work remotely with English-speaking businesses internationally. We agree meeting times, a shared place for working documents and the people who can make decisions. Discovery uses recent examples and walkthroughs with your team. Access, testing, acceptance and handover are agreed for the scope.
