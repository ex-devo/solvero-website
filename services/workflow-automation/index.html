Workflow automation and systems integration.

Connect your systems and automate agreed workflow steps so people can spend less time copying information and chasing updates. We define the behaviour, implement the connection and test the handoff with your team.

Discuss your process challenge

Find the cause of repeated work and delays.

Approvals stall, information gets entered twice and teams chase updates across disconnected tools. We investigate where those problems begin before agreeing what to change.

We follow real examples through the process with the people involved. We check the decisions, handoffs and system dependencies, then define the improvement and how to assess it. Start with a Business Improvement Review, explore Process & Workflow Improvement, or define the technical work through Solution Design & Delivery.

Make everyday processes easier to complete.

We combine process changes with systems integration and automation where they address the problem. These are examples of the work we can help you improve.

Approvals and requests

Reduce time spent chasing decisions. We clarify approval authority, remove redundant steps and route requests to the right person with the information they need. See our approval workflow guide.

Documents and onboarding

Make it clear when a customer is ready for the next step. We define required information and ownership, introduce completeness checks and connect the handoffs. See our customer onboarding guide.

Connected customer information

Reduce repeated data entry and conflicting records. We agree field ownership and matching rules, then implement and test the connection between systems. See our CRM and accounting integration guide.

Recurring reports and updates

Spend less time rebuilding the same report. We trace its inputs, simplify preparation and automate suitable collection and checks, with missing information brought to the owner for review.

A clear route from process review to implementation.

  1. 01

    Analyse and define

    Map how the work happens using recent examples, including a case that went wrong. Investigate repeated steps and waiting, then agree which change is worth making.

    You get a prioritised improvement, a defined scope and clear acceptance criteria. We agree responsibilities, access and measures before implementation.

  2. 02

    Build and test

    Introduce the revised process and implement the agreed tool, integration or automation. Work with the process owner to test the expected result.

    Check missing information, duplicate requests and failed updates too. You get a working change checked against the agreed requirements.

  3. 03

    Hand over and measure

    Document how the workflow operates and who owns its decisions, exceptions and support. Help the people involved put the change into everyday use.

    Review measures such as handling time, waiting and rework against the agreed baseline. You get a clear handover and a way to assess the operational result.

Choose the right change for your process.

Some problems improve when an unnecessary step is removed or a decision has a clear owner. Where software is needed, we assess your existing systems and access requirements, then design the integration, automation or tool around the agreed result. Platforms such as n8n are among the delivery options.

AI can support suitable tasks such as document classification or preparing information for review. We assess the inputs, define checks on its output and keep decision authority clear. Our AI and rules-based automation guide explains how we assess the fit.

Planning the work with your team.

Do you implement the improvements?

Yes. We investigate the process, define the change and deliver the agreed implementation through testing and handover. The scope makes responsibilities and ongoing ownership clear.

Can we start with one workflow?

Yes. Start with one process that creates repeated work or delays. We agree its boundaries and dependencies so the first improvement is practical to deliver and assess.

What will our team need to contribute?

We agree the people, information and access needed for the project. Your process owner helps explain the workflow, resolve questions and check the result.

How much will it cost, and how long will it take?

We assess the process boundary, system access, exceptions and delivery responsibilities before proposing the work and commercial terms. Our automation project cost guide explains what affects the scope.

Can you work with us internationally?

Yes. We are based in Johannesburg and work remotely with English-speaking businesses internationally. We agree the collaboration, access and handover arrangements for your team. Our offering spans industries, with banking and insurance among our areas of experience.
