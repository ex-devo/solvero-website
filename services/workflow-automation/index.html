Workflow automation and process improvement.

Reduce repeated data entry, slow approvals and manual handoffs. Solvero analyses your business workflows and implements practical automation and integrations.

Discuss your workflow

Where does the work get stuck?

A process can work on paper and still require constant attention. People chase approvals, reconcile conflicting records and move the same information between systems.

We start by examining the workflow: what triggers it, who owns each step, which systems it touches and what happens when something goes wrong. That gives the implementation a clear purpose.

Workflows we can help you improve.

These examples show where process analysis, integration and automation can work together. The right scope depends on your process and systems.

Approvals and requests

Capture the information needed to make a decision, route work to the right person and track what is waiting. Define how exceptions, delegations and overdue decisions are handled.

Documents and onboarding

Collect required documents, check for missing information and prepare records for review. Keep human approval where a decision requires judgement.

Connected customer information

Connect records across the systems your teams use. Establish which system owns each field and how to handle duplicate records or conflicting updates.

Recurring reports and updates

Bring recurring inputs together, check their completeness and prepare updates for the people who need them. Surface missing data and failed updates for review.

From understanding the process to implementing the change.

  1. 01

    Analyse and define

    Map the workflow, its decisions and exceptions. Identify the source of delays or repeated work and agree the improvement to pursue.

    Define the scope, responsibilities, system access and measures before implementation begins.

  2. 02

    Build and test

    Implement the agreed changes, including integrations and automation where suitable. Test representative cases with your process owner.

    Include missing information, duplicate requests and failed updates in the design, alongside the expected flow.

  3. 03

    Hand over and measure

    Agree the documentation, ownership and support arrangements needed to operate the workflow.

    Choose measures such as handling time, waiting time and rework. Establish a baseline so the change can be assessed.

Build around the work and the systems you use.

We assess your existing systems, available integrations and access requirements before choosing an implementation approach. Tools such as n8n can connect steps across applications; the process and its operating requirements guide the choice.

AI may help with tasks such as classifying documents or preparing information for review. A clearer process or a standard automation may be enough. We assess where AI would be useful and which decisions need human judgement.

Before we start.

Do you implement the improvements?

Yes. We provide analysis and implementation, with the work and responsibilities defined in the project scope.

Can we start with one workflow?

Yes. Start with a recurring process that creates manual work, delays or rework. We assess its boundaries and dependencies before agreeing the scope.

What will our team need to contribute?

We agree the people, information and access needed for the project. Your process owner helps explain the workflow, resolve questions and check the result.

How much will it cost, and how long will it take?

Scope depends on the process, systems, information and changes involved. We agree the work and commercial terms before that work begins.

Do you only work with financial-services businesses?

No. Our workflow improvement and automation offering serves businesses across industries. Financial services is one specialist area within that broader offering.

Which workflow needs to work better?

Tell us what happens now and where work slows down. A brief description is enough to start the conversation.
