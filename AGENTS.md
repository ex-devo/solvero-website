# Solvero content guidance

Before drafting, editing, reviewing or refining Solvero content, read the relevant writing guide and the positioning and evidence requirements in `README.md`:

- Website marketing copy: `docs/website-copy-guide.md`.
- Articles: `docs/article-writing-guide.md`.

Use the relevant guide for the content's metadata, captions and calls to action as well.

Apply the confirmed requirements and check the finished copy against them. Respect the guide's review status: unanswered interview recommendations and proposed copy remain proposals until the user agrees them.
